# Business Ops Recipe

Use this recipe when assessing a business workflow for automation, reporting, SOP cleanup, or internal AI assistants.

## Goal

Create a practical operations map and identify where AI or automation can reduce manual work.

## Inputs

- Department or workflow area
- Current tools
- Repeated tasks
- Reports and dashboards
- Documents and SOPs
- People involved
- Pain points and delays

## Workflow

1. Map the workflow from start to finish.
2. Identify manual handoffs and bottlenecks.
3. List systems of record and data sources.
4. Find automation opportunities.
5. Separate quick wins from larger platform work.
6. Define an implementation roadmap.
7. Create success metrics.

## Recommended Skills

- `interview-me`
- `context-engineering`
- `spec-driven-development`
- `planning-and-task-breakdown`
- `observability-and-instrumentation`
- `documentation-and-adrs`

## Output Format

```md
# Business Operations Assessment

## Workflow Map

## Current Tools

## Manual Work

## Bottlenecks

## Automation Opportunities

## Quick Wins

## Roadmap

## Success Metrics
```

## Quality Gate

Every recommendation must tie back to a measurable business outcome such as saved time, reduced errors, faster reporting, or better customer response.
