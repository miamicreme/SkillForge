# Client Discovery Recipe

Use this recipe when assessing a business for AI automation, internal tools, reporting, or workflow modernization.

## Goal

Turn a vague request into a clear business problem, scope, roadmap, and proposal-ready plan.

## Inputs

- Industry and team size
- Current tools and systems
- Pain points
- Manual workflows
- Data sources
- Timeline and budget range

## Workflow

1. Clarify the business outcome.
2. Map the current workflow.
3. Identify repetitive work and reporting bottlenecks.
4. Separate a 30-day MVP from the future-state vision.
5. Define scope, boundaries, assumptions, and risks.
6. Produce proposal inputs and next steps.

## Discovery Questions

- What process takes too long today?
- Where do mistakes or delays happen?
- What data is most important to operations?
- Which reports or updates are created manually?
- What would a successful first version accomplish in 30 days?

## Recommended Skills

- `interview-me`
- `idea-refine`
- `spec-driven-development`
- `planning-and-task-breakdown`
- `documentation-and-adrs`

## Output Format

```md
# Client Discovery Summary

## Business Problem

## Current Workflow

## Pain Points

## Automation Opportunities

## Recommended MVP

## Scope

## Risks

## Proposal Outline
```

## Quality Gate

Do not propose implementation until the outcome, current workflow, constraints, and boundaries are clear.
