# SignalBrief Research Pack

The SignalBrief Research Pack converts market and social intelligence into usable business artifacts.

It is designed to work with [`miamicreme/signalbrief`](https://github.com/miamicreme/signalbrief) as the evidence-gathering layer and SkillForge as the workflow/delivery layer.

## Best Customers

- AI consultants
- Automation agencies
- Founders
- Sales teams
- Recruiters
- Product builders
- Real estate and business dealmakers

## Use Cases

| Use Case | Output |
|---|---|
| Company research | Company SignalBrief, call prep, risk notes, outreach angles |
| Competitor research | Side-by-side comparison, positioning gaps, feature opportunities |
| Buyer pain research | Complaint clusters, pain language, MVP opportunities |
| Founder research | Meeting notes, talking points, recent activity summary |
| Deal research | Demand signals, risk notes, buyer angles, local sentiment |
| Trend research | Market movement, cultural signal, content angles |

## Input Checklist

Before running the pack, capture:

- target entity or topic,
- goal of the research,
- target audience,
- decision the user needs to make,
- timeframe if relevant,
- known constraints,
- whether the output should become a proposal, MVP spec, outreach, or briefing note.

## Agent Workflow

1. Run or request a SignalBrief-style research pass.
2. Identify source quality, gaps, and assumptions.
3. Cluster the findings into decision themes.
4. Translate the themes into business implications.
5. Produce the selected output format.
6. Add verification notes and unanswered questions.

## Output Formats

- Executive brief
- Sales-call prep
- Discovery question list
- Consulting proposal outline
- MVP opportunity map
- Outreach angle sheet
- Deal intelligence summary
- Competitive positioning memo

## Quality Gates

A SignalBrief Research Pack output is not done until it includes:

- the decision it supports,
- the key signals found,
- the strength of the evidence,
- assumptions and gaps,
- recommended next action,
- a client-ready summary.

## Default Framing

SignalBrief finds the truth outside the building. SkillForge turns it into a plan, pitch, product, or delivery path.
